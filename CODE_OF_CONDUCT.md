# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in LocalView welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Welcome different technical perspectives.
- Keep feedback focused on the work and the user problem.
- Respect privacy and do not share private project data or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Please report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
